Argentine Tango Milonga in Landgraaf

Las Tres Esquinas is an Argentine tango milonga in Landgraaf, Limburg, Netherlands, bringing dancers together for social tango, workshops, live tango music and traditional milonga nights.

Come early for the workshop, stay for the orchestra and dance until the last tanda. If you have never been to a milonga before, you are welcome here too.

Tango Workshop in Landgraaf

Cinthya Tomino & Luciana Sacchetti19:30All levels

Cinthya Tomino
Luciana Sacchetti

Playing with space and dynamics

The same movement can feel completely different depending on its size and energy. We explore the spectrum of dynamics and sizes for many elements.

An hour of practice before the milonga: walking, timing and a comfortable embrace, so you feel ready when the dancing starts.

More about the teachers

Luciana Sacchetti

Dancer, artistic gymnast, athlete and circus artist, she approached tango 15 years ago in Mar del Plata, Argentina. She immediately began taking lessons with internationally renowned masters and artists, and still today she travels to Europe and Argentina to deepen her knowledge of this dance more and more. She is invited to Argentina, Italy, the United States and the Netherlands to teach and perform with various artists.

Cinthya Tomino

Professional tango dancer from Argentina. Starting her journey in tango as a child, Cinthya began her artistic formation at a young age, studying with some of the most renowned tango masters in Buenos Aires and developing her expertise in both roles, as a leader and a follower. After performing in some of the most prestigious theaters and renowned milongas in Buenos Aires, she has spent more than 15 years teaching and dancing in different countries, sharing the traditions, culture, and passion of Argentine tango, as a teacher, dancer, and tango DJ.

Milonga El Colectivo

Cinthya Tomino and Luciana Sacchetti are part of El Colectivo, a tango project in Rotterdam that runs the “El Colectivo” milonga — a monthly gathering devoted to Argentine tango, with traditional music, dancing and a space for the tango community to meet. It takes place on the fourth Friday of every month in Rotterdam.

The people behind the milonga

Maddie & Joel (NL / AR)

Maddie and Joel run Tango MJ in Limburg, organising Argentine tango classes, coaching, workshops and social tango events in the region. They are growing the tango community here, with regular classes every Friday at 19:30 at de Brandpoort.

They created Las Tres Esquinas to bring the milongueros of the region together in one beautiful, friendly place. Come dance, stay late, come back.

Argentine tango in Limburg

Milonga in Landgraaf, Limburg

Las Tres Esquinas is an Argentine tango milonga in Landgraaf, Limburg, Netherlands. We bring together tango dancers from Landgraaf, Heerlen, Kerkrade, Brunssum, Maastricht and the surrounding South Limburg region.

Our milonga combines social Argentine tango dancing, traditional tango music, guest DJs, live orchestras and tango workshops in a friendly local community.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is the tango milonga in Landgraaf?

Las Tres Esquinas takes place at Zalencentrum de Brandpoort, Hoogstraat 191, 6373 HS Landgraaf, Limburg, Netherlands.

Is Las Tres Esquinas a milonga for beginners?

Yes. The workshop before the milonga is open to all levels, and if you have never been to a milonga before, you are welcome here too.

Where can I dance Argentine tango in Limburg?

Las Tres Esquinas is organised by Tango MJ, which also runs regular Argentine tango classes every Friday at 19:30 at de Brandpoort in Landgraaf, Limburg.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, there is free parking on the square directly in front of Zalencentrum de Brandpoort.
