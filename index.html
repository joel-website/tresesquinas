The night

A milonga with live music

Las Tres Esquinas is a night of Argentine tango in Landgraaf, with a live orchestra and a friendly floor. Nothing fancy, just good music and room to dance.

Come early for the workshop, stay for the orchestra and dance until the last tanda. If you have never been to a milonga before, you are welcome here too.

Live music

Orquesta Conjunto Margot

Two sets · 21:15 and 22:45

Conjunto Margot is a collection of amateur musicians who are long-time Argentine Tango fans and, from that passion, started to play and further discover the music they already love.

At milongas we play with other (some professional) tango-loving musicians.

Tonight's DJ

Cinthya Tomino (AR / NL)

Milonga from 20:30Until 01:00

Cinthya began her journey as a tango DJ in Argentina, starting out in her hometown before becoming a professional in Buenos Aires, playing in the city's top milongas.

For more than 15 years, she has traveled the world sharing her DJ sets and the authentic porteño style.

The people behind the milonga

Maddie & Joel

Maddie and Joel run Tango MJ in Limburg: classes, coaching and events around Argentine tango.

Las Tres Esquinas is their invitation to the wider scene. Come dance, stay late, come back.
