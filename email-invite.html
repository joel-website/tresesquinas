Milonga Las Tres Esquinas

Friday · 6 November 2026 · Landgraaf, Netherlands

Hi there,

Thank you so much for joining us at Milonga Las Tres Esquinas in May. It was a special night for us — behind every detail that day there was a lot of work, a lot of energy, a lot of happiness. We had been dreaming of it for months, and thanks to all of you, we got to see that dream come true. Along the way we also learned a great deal: we listened to your experiences, your tips and your suggestions for the next night of dancing.

If you'd like to tell us how the night was for you, you can do so here:

Share your experience →

And here we are — with even more enthusiasm and happiness — bringing you another night of dancing, a night to remember, with guest teachers, guest DJs and live musicians. We want to share this dream with you again. It isn't only our dream: it belongs to everyone who wants a better world — a world with more embraces, more laughter, more friends. A world where tango is danced on every corner, where everyone gets the chance to share something that was born in Buenos Aires and now travels the whole world.

We hope to have you with us, with your good energy. And if you can't make it, we'd love for you to pass this along to anyone you think might be interested. Together, let's help tango grow in this region of three corners, three countries, three languages — but one heart.
